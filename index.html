How to play

Play in any order. Complete all four to finish the set.

Decipher: Find the five-letter word in six tries. Green means the right spot, gold means another spot, gray means absent.

Gather: Select three connected words from the mixed board, then choose Gather trio. Find four trios; four intruders stay unused. You have four mistakes. Connection hints are optional.

Distill: Select a bottle, then a destination. Pour onto the same color or into an empty bottle. Separate each color into a full bottle. Symbols distinguish colors.

Inscribe: Drag through neighboring letters to find three words matching the clue. Every four other valid, unique words (three or more letters) reveal a random letter in a random unfinished word. Release to check. You can also tap letters and choose Check word. Diagonals count. Drag back one tile to undo a step.

No time limit. Each set saves separately.
