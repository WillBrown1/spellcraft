How to play

Play in any order. Complete all four to finish the set.

Spellword: Find the five-letter word in six tries. Green means the right spot, gold means another spot, gray means absent.

Kindred: Find four groups of four connected words. You have four mistakes.

Water Sort: Select a bottle, then a destination. Pour onto the same color or into an empty bottle. Separate each color into a full bottle. Symbols distinguish colors.

Grimoire: Drag through neighboring letters to find three words matching the clue. Release to check. You can also tap letters and choose Check word. Diagonals count. Drag back one tile to undo a step.

No time limit. Each set saves separately.
